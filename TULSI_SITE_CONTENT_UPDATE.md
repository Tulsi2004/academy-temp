This is a **content edit**, not a redesign. Keep the existing visual system,
layout, spacing and animations. Change copy, fix links, add one new section.

## What this page is for

This is the parent-company site. It is **not** the Academy OS sales page —
that lives at `academy-os-landing.vercel.app` and does the selling.

This page has three jobs:

1. Make TULSI look like a real company to anyone who searches it
2. Send interested visitors to Academy OS
3. Tell people who is behind it

Everything that doesn't serve one of those three should go.

## Priority 4 — Remove unbacked claims

These promise things no shipped product does yet. A visitor who later sees the
beta will notice the gap.

**Delete:**

- "We don't digitize businesses. We transform how they operate."
  → Replace with: *"We build software that fits how a business already works,
    instead of asking it to change."*

- The **Intelligence** pillar in "What we do" ("We turn business data into
  insights that help people make better decisions")
  → Replace with **Support**: *"We stay involved after launch. The product
    keeps changing because the people using it keep telling us what's wrong."*

- The value **"Intelligence, Not Information"**
  → Cut. Restore it when the product does it.

**Reduce the six values to four.** Keep: Workflow First, Simplicity Wins,
Partnership Over Support, Speak Their Language. Cut Build an Ecosystem
(too abstract for a visitor) and Intelligence Not Information (unbacked).
